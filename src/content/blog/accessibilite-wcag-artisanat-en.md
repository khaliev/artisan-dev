## Universal Access to Web Information

A high-quality website must be accessible to everyone without discrimination, regardless of device, assistive tech, or individual ability.

Web accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA) is not just a regulatory checklist: it is a foundational pillar of software quality and digital ethics.

### Key Implementation Rules

1. **100% Keyboard Navigable**: Visible focus indicators and logical tab order.
2. **Verified Color Contrast**: Minimum 4.5:1 ratio across light and dark themes.
3. **Flawless Semantic Structure**: Strict heading hierarchy and descriptive `alt` tags.
