## Context & Challenge

DEZAL CONSTRUCTION is a general building company based in Reims, specializing in house masonry, interior and exterior renovation, extension and outdoor development work.

The company needed a clear online presence to present its services, showcase its references (ALDI and LIDL stores, Espace Habitat housing, CAP WEST hotel) and let prospects request a free quote.

## Solution

- **Structured business website**: home, company presentation, team, downloadable brochure and contact page.
- **Highlighted services**: structural work, finishing work, houses, renovation, slabs, walls, renderings, tiling, plasterboard and painting.
- **Local focus**: service areas (Reims, Metz, Paris, Strasbourg, Luxembourg, etc.) and a "Free quote" call to action.
