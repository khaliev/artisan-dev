## Context & Challenge

As part of the energy transition plan in the Grand Est region of France, local authorities needed an accessible digital tool allowing residents to evaluate their home energy efficiency.

The core challenge was to provide a smooth user experience while maintaining a minimal carbon footprint for the website itself.

## Problem

Existing energy calculators were overly complex, laden with third-party trackers, and shipped megabytes of JavaScript, hurting performance on rural mobile networks.

## Solution

- **100% Static Architecture** built with Astro for instant page loads.
- **Zero client-side JS framework** for consultation pages.
- **Warm editorial design** focused on readability and WCAG AA accessibility.
